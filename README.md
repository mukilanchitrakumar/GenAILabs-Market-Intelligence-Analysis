# GenAI Labs — Market Intelligence Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an evidence-based **Generative AI Market Intelligence Analysis** developed using **Amazon Quick** and a full market dataset containing **19,329 Generative AI product records**.

The project combines structured dataset analysis, AI-assisted market research, competitive intelligence, visual analysis, verification, and executive-level recommendations.

The objective was to transform raw Generative AI product data and external research signals into a structured market intelligence brief while maintaining clear evidence, verification, uncertainty, and decision boundaries.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The analysis investigates the Generative AI product landscape across:

- Product release trends
- Primary market domains
- Intelligence types
- Domain × intelligence-type relationships
- Pricing models
- API availability
- Video and audio product signals
- External market and competitive research

The analysis focuses on **observable product-level evidence** and does not treat product counts as direct measures of revenue, market share, profitability, customer demand, or commercial opportunity.

---

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** `Generative AI Market & Competitive Intelligence Dataset.csv`

**Records analyzed:** **19,329**

**Release-year range:** **2022–2025**

The dataset was analyzed using the **full dataset rather than a sample**.

Key analytical dimensions include:

- `Release_Year`
- `Primary_Domain`
- `Intelligence_Type`
- `Pricing_Model`
- `API_Availability`

---

## 🔬 Analysis & Research Workflow

The project followed an evidence-first workflow:

1. Dataset uploaded and validated in Amazon Quick.
2. Full-dataset statistics were generated and verified.
3. Market distributions and cross-tabulations were analyzed.
4. Visualizations were created to communicate major patterns.
5. Amazon Quick Research was used for external market intelligence.
6. Research outputs and source evidence were captured through screenshots.
7. An AI research agent was created to support structured market analysis.
8. Findings were reconciled against the verified dataset.
9. Confidence, limitations, risks, and non-conclusions were documented.
10. The final Market Intelligence Brief was prepared for submission.

## ⚠️ Analytical Boundaries

Product counts in this project represent **observed product listings in the dataset**.

They should **not** be interpreted as direct evidence of:

- Revenue
- Market share
- Profitability
- Customer demand
- Product success
- Commercial opportunity

Strategic implications are therefore framed within the limits of the available evidence.
